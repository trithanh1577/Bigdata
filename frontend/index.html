FROM THE COLLECTION
KNOW YOUR SOURCES

Good discovery starts with clear data.

Recipe Atlas is the website component of the Global Culinary Knowledge Base, implemented by Nguyễn Trần Hoàng Nhân (2474802010272).

Live recipe records preserve each provider's attribution, source links, license or terms, and collection time. Versions from different providers remain separate. Missing cooking times, difficulty, allergens, nutrition, or substitutions are shown as unavailable, never invented.

Ingredient exclusion is a text-matching aid. It cannot identify every synonym, hidden ingredient, or cross-contact risk. Missing allergen declarations mean unknown.
